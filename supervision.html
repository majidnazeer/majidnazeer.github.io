Supervision and teaching summary

Majid specialises in teaching remote sensing and geospatial analysis to students from varied technical backgrounds, translating satellite and sensor data into practical environmental applications.

At the undergraduate level, Majid is Subject Leader for a core course Remote Sensing at PolyU since 2023. Previously, he has served as teaching assistant for undergraduate courses Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing and Geo-statistics and Exploratory Data Analysis at PolyU. He draws on his own active research projects to bring current, real-world datasets and case studies into the classroom, giving students direct exposure to operational environmental monitoring problems.

At the postgraduate level, he has instructed courses in Advanced GIS, Geo-Statistical Analysis, and GIS for Health Informatics. He has supervised numerous MPhil, MSc, and BSc research projects to completion, and currently supervises a cohort of PhD, MPhil, MSc, and BSc students, including as chief supervisor for three PhD candidates. He regularly sits on departmental panels for PhD student recruitment and final-year project evaluation.

Majid is also a team member on Revolutionizing Higher Education through Innovative and Emerging Technologies in Flipped Learning, a HK$2,000,000 project funded by the University Grants Committee of Hong Kong's Fund for Innovative Technology-in-Education (2023-2026).
