Bio

Dr Majid Nazeer works at the interface of remote sensing, coastal environmental science and artificial intelligence — building satellite, airborne and ground-based methods to monitor coastal water quality, blue carbon ecosystems and urban environments under climate change.

Most of his fieldwork and modelling sits in the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area, though the atmospheric correction routines, spectral libraries and retrieval algorithms developed here travel well beyond it. He holds a PhD from PolyU (2016) and currently supervises PhD, MPhil and undergraduate researchers — see Supervision and teaching.
