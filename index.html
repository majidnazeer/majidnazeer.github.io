Research Assistant Professor, PhD

Majid Nazeer

Department of Land Surveying and Geospatial Science
The Hong Kong Polytechnic University

Dr Majid Nazeer works at the interface of remote sensing, coastal environmental science and artificial intelligence - building satellite, airborne and ground-based methods to monitor coastal water quality, blue carbon ecosystems and urban environments under climate change.

Most of his fieldwork and modelling sits in the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area, though the atmospheric correction routines, spectral libraries and retrieval algorithms developed here travel well beyond it. He holds a PhD from PolyU (2016) and currently supervises PhD, MPhil and undergraduate researchers see CV.

Focus areas

Four connected lines of work. The sensors and correction methods are shared — only the water, leaf or rooftop changes.

01

Blue carbon & coastal ecosystems

Mapping and monitoring mangrove, saltmarsh and seagrass systems ? including a hyperspectral library for Hong Kong mangrove species and leaf decay dynamics.

02

Water quality & aquatic remote sensing

Retrieval of chlorophyll-a, turbidity and water clarity in optically complex Case-II coastal waters, where atmospheric correction dominates the error budget.

03

AI & machine learning for EO

Learned retrieval models, systematic reviews of AI in geospatial practice, and when machine learning genuinely outperforms physically based methods.

04

Forest and Urban Vegetation Remote Sensing

Spectral libraries for crops and urban trees, canopy mapping, and thermal behaviour of vegetated and built surfaces across land-use and climate zones.
