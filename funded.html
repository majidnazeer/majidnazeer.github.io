Research summary

Majid's primary interest is the application of remote sensing and geospatial data to inform environmental monitoring and management decisions. His expertise spans satellite, airborne, and field-based sensing, machine learning, and interdisciplinary collaboration with government and industry partners. He has worked across ecosystems ranging from coastal and marine waters to terrestrial and urban forests. These interests mean he often partners with government agencies to provide analyses that inform environmental planning - examples include operational coastal water quality monitoring platforms for Hong Kong EPD, hyperspectral and LiDAR-based urban tree health assessments, and habitat mapping to support blue carbon and nature-based solutions planning.

Majid is also interested in fundamental research that advances the quantitative tools available for environmental monitoring. This includes developing new spectral indices and atmospheric correction methods for satellite-based water quality retrieval, building spectral libraries for vegetation and crop species, and applying AI-based methods to map forest biomass, canopy structure, and land cover change.
