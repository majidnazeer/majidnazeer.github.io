Research summary

Remote sensing, coastal water quality, blue carbon ecosystems, mangroves and wetlands, atmospheric correction, spectral libraries, AI for Earth observation, urban and forest canopy, Greater Bay Area.

Dr Majid Nazeer’s research develops satellite, airborne and ground-based methods to monitor coastal water quality, blue carbon systems and vegetated landscapes under climate change. Work centres on the Guangdong–Hong Kong–Macao Greater Bay Area, with algorithms and libraries that transfer to other coasts and cities.

Funding supports projects as Principal Investigator (including RGC GRF blue-carbon work and PolyU SLIVES), Co-Investigator on collaborative environmental and geospatial awards, and key researcher roles on major consortium grants — plus a UGC teaching innovation award.
